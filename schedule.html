04 / Event schedule

The full HackMerced flow.

A full-day rhythm of check-in, building, workshops, meals, and community moments designed to keep the energy moving all day long.

9:00 AM

Check-in + team formation

Doors open, attendees arrive, and teams begin meeting and finalizing project ideas.

10:00 AM

Breakfast

Early arrivals fuel up and settle in before the first wave of workshops and building starts.

11:00 AM

Workshop kickoff

Teams jump into beginner-friendly sessions, hands-on demos, and mentor guidance.

11:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Lunch

Take a break, recharge, and connect with other participants before the afternoon push.

1:00 PM

Build sprint

Project work continues with rapid iteration, debugging, and team collaboration.

3:00 PM

Mentor office hours

Students get one-on-one support, feedback, and technical guidance for their prototypes.

5:00 PM - 6:00 PM

Dinner

Everyone reconnects for a shared meal and a quick reset before final presentations and demos.

6:30 PM

Showcase + awards

Teams share what they built, celebrate the weekend, and recognize standout work.
